How to read this

Fair is the price with no sportsbook margin, averaged across the other books that offer the same line. Edge is your expected profit per dollar at the listed price if that fair number is right. Stake is a fraction of the Kelly bet, capped at your max.

A row marked estimate means your book has a different number than the other books, for example 38.5 yards when the rest are at 42.5. The fair price there is worked out from how much that stat usually varies, so it is rougher than a same-line comparison and the stake is halved. Touchdown and interception props are left out of this check.

Before betting, open the book and confirm the line and price still match. Props move fast and the feed can lag by a minute or more. An amber edge (8% or higher) usually means a stale line, an injury the other books have already reacted to, or different bet rules, so check the news first.

An empty board is a normal result. It means no book is out of line with the market right now.
